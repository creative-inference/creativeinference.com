Inside the Engine: How BarDC Runs

Step through the four production systems powering Bar DC — our flagship self-updating Washington, D.C. nightlife platform — and the shared blueprint behind our upcoming releases.

01 · Autonomous Content Operations

Every Night, 25+ Specialized Agents Rebuild the City

While Washington sleeps, an orchestrated pipeline crawls hundreds of bar websites, extracts live happenings, monitors local press, verifies domain health, writes the morning dispatch, and ships a fresh static build before 6 AM.

  • Hijacked & Expired Domain Guards: Headless crawlers detect parked domains, casino redirects, and DNS failures automatically so dead links never pollute listings.
  • Multi-Model Structured Extraction: Local open-weights reasoning models (including Inkling and Gemma, with speculative decoding and schema-enforced JSON output) turn messy HTML menus and calendars into typed deals, recurring nights, and DJ sets with automated QA.
  • RSS Radar & Editorial Synthesis: Agents scan DC journalism feeds daily to onboard openings, verify closures, synthesize multi-source venue dossiers, and author the morning "Tonight in DC" dispatch.

02 · Multimodal Quality Control & Generative Art

Vision-Verified Photography Meets Bespoke Cinemagraphs

Automated directories usually look "scraped" — full of stretched logos, DJ flyers, and generic AI slop. We solved visual quality from both ends: strict multimodal vision filtering for real venue photos and curated 2D print styles for editorial media.

  • 335+ Vision-Verified Venue Heroes: Candidate images scraped from official bar websites are audited locally by multimodal vision-language models (such as Inkling and Gemma) — approving clean interiors, rooftops, and cocktails while rejecting overlaid text, flyers, or collages.
  • Zero-Thrash Local GPU Batching: Nightly and weekly media jobs coordinate automatic VRAM handoff between local LLMs and ComfyUI — batching Flux stills and Wan image-to-video WebM loops for lightweight atmospheric motion.
  • Admin Override Controls: React ops dashboard lets editors lock, swap, or re-verify hero photography with one click.

04 · Built for Humans & External AI Agents

Machine-Readable Feeds, /llms.txt & Streamable MCP

Next-generation discovery happens inside AI assistants as often as search engines. Our platform architecture exposes structured static JSON feeds, a canonical /llms.txt manifest, and a read-only Streamable HTTP Model Context Protocol server module.

  • Zero-Auth Machine Feeds: Build-time search.json (636+ venues), tonight.json, map.json, and city.json serve structured context at CDN speed without scraping HTML.
  • 4-Tool Streamable HTTP MCP Module: Exposes venue discovery, tonight's live happenings, neighborhood happy hours, and walkable 3-stop Night Pass builders over JSON-RPC 2.0.
  • Rich Schema Graph: Consolidated neighborhood hubs, 301 canonicalization, and BarOrPub / Event / Article JSON-LD keep search engines and LLMs aligned.
